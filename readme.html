Attendance·Tracker
Chrome extension · Google Meet

Attendance on a presence timeline.

Attendance Tracker tracks who joined your Google Meet and when — plotting every join and leave on the meeting clock, telling you how much of the meeting each person was actually there for, and rolling recurring sessions up into a series.

What it does

  • Presence timeline — every join & leave, per participant, on the meeting clock
  • Series — bundle a recurring training and read the attendance matrix across sessions; click a person for their exact times
  • Who was missing — an expected-attendee roster marks absences, per meeting or per series
  • Analytics — filter by date, series or meeting and read the slice as numbers, charts and tables
  • PDF reports — a technical report for one meeting or a whole series, with every event
  • CSV & JSON — export to a spreadsheet or back it up, and import it all again
  • Dark mode — follows your browser, with a manual override
  • English & Polish, and all data stays on your device

Install (developer mode)

  1. Clone from GitHub
  2. Open chrome://extensions and enable Developer mode
  3. Click “Load unpacked” and select the project folder
